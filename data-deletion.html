Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: August 31, 2026

You can request the permanent deletion of all personal data AmanCode holds about you at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Via any messaging channel you used with us (WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, or Telegram): send us the text "delete my data" — or in Arabic, «حذف بياناتي». Our assistant will confirm your request on that channel.
  2. By email: write to amancode.tech@gmail.com from an account linked to your previous conversations, including a short statement of what to delete.
  3. Facebook-initiated: if you connected through Facebook/Messenger, you may also remove our app access from your Facebook settings; we honor those removal signals within 72 hours.

What gets deleted

  • Your name, phone number, and conversation history in our database.
  • Any proposal drafts or contact records.
  • Deletion is permanent and completed within 30 days of request.
